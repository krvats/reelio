Copyright and takedown policy

Reelio respects creators. Users must only use videos they own or have permission to use.

Report a violation

Message Instagram @kr5na_ with: your name and contact details; a description of the work; the link to the content; a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised; a statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner; your signature.

What we do

We promptly pause the post or the flow involved and tell the account holder. If you believe a report was a mistake you may send a counter-notice with your details and a good-faith statement. Accounts with repeated valid complaints are closed.
