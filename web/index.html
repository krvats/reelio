Questions.

Do I need to keep my phone or computer on?

No. Reelio runs in the cloud and posts by itself.

Which accounts work?

Instagram professional accounts (creator or business) and YouTube channels, through their official connections.

Is it allowed by Instagram and YouTube?

Reelio uses their official publishing connections only. It does not scrape accounts or use unofficial logins. You are responsible for posting only videos you own or may use.

Where are my logins kept?

They are encrypted with a passphrase only you know before they are saved, and they are never shown again in the app.

How many posts a day can I schedule?

You choose, up to Instagram's limit of 100 a day per account. YouTube's own upload quota applies to YouTube.

What if YouTube blocks the download?

Reelio tries your saved cookies in turn, rests the ones that fail, and falls back to a backup Drive folder. If everything fails it tells you exactly what to add.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The live demo has no sign-up and uses sample data.
