Reelio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Reelio is a personal tool that posts your own videos to your own Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What we access

With your permission, Reelio reads video files from your Google Drive folders and uploads videos to your YouTube channel. It also uses your Instagram account to publish posts.

Where your data is stored

Reelio has no server and no database of its own. Your settings and login tokens are stored in a private GitHub repository owned by you, with the tokens encrypted by a passphrase only you know. Videos are processed temporarily on GitHub's runners and deleted afterwards.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Reelio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect Reelio at any time in the app, delete your private data repository, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
